# Tata Data Visualisation: Strategic Revenue Analysis 📊

**Role:** Aspiring Data Analyst  
**Program:** Tata Group Virtual Experience Program (via Forage)  

## 🎯 Project Objective
This project focuses on analyzing an online retail dataset to uncover the patterns that dictate strategic business moves. The objective is to provide actionable insights to executive leadership regarding overarching revenue trends, geographic expansion opportunities, and customer loyalty behaviors, ultimately enabling data-driven planning for the upcoming year.

## 🧠 The Analyst's Approach
I focus on building scalable IT solutions through logic and precision. My approach to this dataset combines a technical foundation in data manipulation with high-level strategic thinking—treating data not just as numbers, but as the underlying logic that dictates a business's "next move." 

By anticipating the distinct needs of the CEO (operations & bottom-line revenue) and the CMO (customer behavior & demand), the resulting dashboard filters out noise and delivers immediate, executive-level clarity.

## 📊 Project Phases

### 1. Framing the Business Scenario
Before visualizing any data, I established 8 core strategic questions to guide the analysis:
* **CEO Priorities:** International revenue opportunities outside the UK, month-over-month revenue trends, risk assessment of order cancellations, and dependency on the top 10% of high-value accounts.
* **CMO Priorities:** Top 10 product performance, revenue contributions from repeat vs. one-time buyers, average order value by demographic, and customer acquisition peaks. 

### 2. Data Cleaning & Preparation
Processed the raw online retail dataset to ensure accuracy in the final visuals. Key transformations included filtering out negative quantities (returns/cancellations) for net-revenue calculations, standardizing geographical data, and calculating custom `Revenue` metrics.

### 3. Interactive Power BI Dashboard
Designed a targeted Power BI dashboard utilizing optimal chart types (Line Charts for non-cyclical temporal data, Bar Charts for categorical comparisons) to directly answer the executive prompts.

*(Note to viewer: You can view the full PDF export of the dashboard metrics in the files above, or download the `.pbix` file to interact with the raw data).*

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* **Geographical Expansion:** Identified specific international regions beyond the UK that present the most profitable growth opportunities.
* **Seasonal Demand:** Mapped out month-over-month revenue trends, highlighting peak operational periods for inventory planning.
* **Customer Retention:** Quantified the financial impact of repeat customers versus one-time buyers, providing a foundation for targeted loyalty campaigns.
